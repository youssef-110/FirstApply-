Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Firstapply processes, why, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). In short: you can browse jobs without an account and without being tracked. If you create an account, we store your email address and the alerts, saved jobs and notes you add, and we use your email address only to sign you in and send the alerts you set up.

2. Visiting the website

When you open Firstapply, your browser sends technical data that our hosting provider needs to deliver the site: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the referring page, and your browser and operating system. This data is processed in server logs to deliver the site and to keep it secure (for example, to detect attacks). It is not combined with other data or used to identify you.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in running a secure and working website. Logs are deleted automatically by the hosting provider after a short period.

We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or social media plugins.

3. Account and sign-in

You can create an account with your email address. There is no password: we email you a one-time link that signs you in. We store:

  • your email address,
  • when you created the account, accepted the terms, last signed in and last received an alert email,
  • a fingerprint (hash) of each sign-in link, and when it expires and was used. The link itself is not stored.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, because this data is needed to provide the account you asked for. Unused sign-in links expire after 15 minutes and are deleted within a day. Your account data is kept until you delete your account.

4. Job alerts, saved jobs and application tracking

If you use these features, we store the search filters of your alerts, which jobs we emailed you and when, the jobs you save or mark as applied, and any notes you write. We use them only to show them back to you and to send you the alerts you created. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. This data is kept until you delete it or your account.

You can turn alert emails off at any time, either on the Alerts page or with the unsubscribe link in every alert email.

5. Protection against abuse

To stop sign-in emails from being used to flood inboxes, and to protect the site's email quota, we briefly record the IP address and email address of sign-in requests and count them. These records are deleted after two days at the latest. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.

6. Cookies and local storage

After you sign in, we set one cookie named fa_session. It keeps you signed in for up to 30 days and contains no information beyond an account number and a security signature. It is strictly necessary for the service you asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG), so no consent is required. Signing out deletes it.

Your browser's local storage keeps your settings on your device: light or dark theme, your last search filters, and when you last visited (to mark new jobs). This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us.

8. Content from other websites

Job listings come from workingstudentjobs.de and the Bundesagentur für Arbeit. We fetch these listings on our server, so no data about you is sent to these sources when you browse.

Company logos are loaded directly from assets.workingstudentjobs.de. When your browser loads a logo, that server receives your IP address, like any website you visit. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our interest in showing recognisable listings. Fonts are served from our own server.

When you press Apply or open an original posting, you leave Firstapply. From then on, the privacy policy of that website applies. We do not tell employers that you visited or applied.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16),
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17),
  • restrict processing (Art. 18),
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).

When signed in, you can download all your data and delete your account yourself on the Alerts page. Deleting your account erases your email address, alerts, saved jobs and notes immediately. For anything else, email us at the address in section 1.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

10. Other

Providing your email address is voluntary. Without it you can still browse and search all jobs, but you can't use alerts, saved jobs or application tracking. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling as defined in Art. 22 GDPR.

We may update this policy when the service changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
